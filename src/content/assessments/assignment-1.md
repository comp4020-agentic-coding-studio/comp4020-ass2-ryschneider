## The brief

> Pick one Latter Day Saint splinter group and argue how the succession
> vacuum, the charismatic claim, the doctrinal pivot, and the institutional
> or legal response together explain why it exists as a separate church.

You may use one of the groups covered in lectures (the Utah church, the
Rigdonites, the Strangites, the Reorganization, the fundamentalist/FLDS
movement, the Cutlerites, the Bickertonites, the Church of Christ Temple Lot)
or another documented Latter Day Saint splinter, as long as you can source
it. The room the brief leaves is in the pivot: most groups make more than one
doctrinal argument for their own legitimacy, and a strong response picks the
one that actually did the work of keeping members, rather than the one the
group states officially.

## What you submit

A single written argument, 1200–1800 words, structured around the four
moves. Cite sources as you go — a claim about what someone said or wrote
needs a source a marker could go and check, not a paraphrase of what "is
generally known."

Marking is weighted: correct use of the framework matters more than volume of
research, but research that's generic (true of every splinter group, not
specifically true of yours) won't carry the second criterion.
