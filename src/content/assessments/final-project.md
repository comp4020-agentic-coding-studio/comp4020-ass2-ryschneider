## The brief

> Build a visual map of the Latter Day Saint movement's splinters —
> founder, founding date, and the succession event each one traces back to —
> and write an analysis connecting at least three of them to this course's
> four-part framework.

The map is the artefact a reader looks at first: a family tree, a timeline,
a diagram, whatever shape best shows how one succession crisis produced this
many separate churches. The analysis is what turns the map from a chart into
an argument — pick three groups whose comparison teaches something the map
alone can't, the way week 9's session compared what different branches kept
and discarded.

Scope note: eight groups in real depth beats fifteen in name only. The
semester covered roughly a dozen; a map that traces a few further branches
you've researched yourself is welcome, but isn't required for full marks.

## What you submit

The map, in whatever format renders clearly (image, PDF, or a linked
interactive page), and the written analysis as a separate document or a
clearly marked section of the same one. Cite sources for any claim in the
analysis the way Assignment 1 required.

This one is marked holistically: there's no criterion table, because what
makes a map like this work is largely a matter of judgement — does it hold
together as a single readable artefact, and does the analysis add insight
the map by itself doesn't already show.
