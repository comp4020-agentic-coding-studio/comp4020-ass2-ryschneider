## Before the session

Read the course outline and the framework it uses all semester: a succession
vacuum, a charismatic claim, a doctrinal pivot, and an institutional or legal
response. Skim the assessment page's list of Latter Day Saint splinter
churches and pick one to follow across the semester — you'll need it for
Assignment 1.

## In the session

A walk through Nauvoo, Illinois in early 1844: the Nauvoo Legion, the Council
of Fifty's secret political ambitions, and the fact that Joseph Smith held
more concurrent titles — mayor, lieutenant general, prophet, presidential
candidate — than any one successor could plausibly inherit at once. We name
the four bodies who will each, within weeks of his death, argue they were the
one he meant to leave it to.

## Afterwards

No mark this week. You leave with the framework and a splinter group of your
own to track — everything from week 2 onward tests it against a real case.
