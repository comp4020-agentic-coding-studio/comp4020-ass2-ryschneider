## Before the session

Read at least two competing eyewitness accounts of the August 8 conference —
some written that week, some decades later — and note where they disagree.

## In the session

Rigdon addresses the congregation first and is received badly; Brigham Young
speaks, and multiple witnesses later describe seeing or hearing something
they read as Joseph Smith's presence in him. We treat the "mantle" claim as a
case study in how a charismatic argument gets made and validated in real
time, in a room, rather than through documents.

## Afterwards

Bring your notes on the eyewitness discrepancies to next week — they matter
for the evidence standard Assignment 1 expects.
