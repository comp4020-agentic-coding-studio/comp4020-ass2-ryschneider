## Before the session

Read the summary of James Strang's claim and coronation. Note the specific
documentary problem historians have found with the letter he produced.

## In the session

Strang produced a letter, postmarked before Smith's death, naming him
successor — and it doesn't hold up under the handwriting and postal-date
scrutiny historians have since applied to it. We use his claim, and his
eventual 1850 coronation as king on Beaver Island, as the sharpest example in
the course of a charismatic claim resting on a forged document rather than a
witnessed moment.
