## Before the session

Read about the 1860 formation of the Reorganized Church around Joseph Smith
III, and about the doctrine of lineal succession it argues for.

## In the session

A different succession theory entirely: authority passes by blood, not by
quorum vote or eyewitness testimony. The Reorganization's founders also
needed a doctrinal pivot to distinguish themselves from Salt Lake, and found
it by denying Joseph Smith Jr. ever taught polygamy at all — building an
entire counter-history on that denial.

## Afterwards

This is the last of the four founding claims we study directly (the Twelve,
Rigdon, Strang, the Reorganization). Next week, Assignment 1 asks you to run
one of them — or your own chosen group — through the framework.
