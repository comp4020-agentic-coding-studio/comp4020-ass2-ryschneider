## Before the session

Bring a full draft of your Assignment 1 argument — the one applying the
four-part framework to your chosen group. It doesn't need to be finished; it
needs to exist.

## In the session

The Utah church's own trajectory, from openly defending plural marriage to
the Morrill and Edmunds–Tucker Acts seizing church property, to the 1890
Manifesto ending the practice — read here as an institution splitting under
external, legal pressure rather than an internal succession crisis, and as an
unusually clean example of the framework's fourth move, institutional
response. The second half of the session is a working review of Assignment 1
drafts.
