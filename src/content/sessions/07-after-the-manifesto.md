## Before the session

Read about the 1890s–1930s fundamentalist movement: the people who read the
Manifesto as a political concession rather than a doctrinal reversal, and
kept practicing plural marriage regardless.

## In the session

This schism has no dead founder. The fundamentalist movement splits from the
mainstream church over a doctrine the mainstream church itself changed —
continuationism, the claim that a valid revelation stays binding even after
the church publicly reverses its position. We trace this to Short Creek
(later Colorado City/Hildale) and the FLDS church, still operating today.

## Afterwards

Hold this case next to week 4's Strangites: one collapsed at its founder's
death, the other has now outlasted the church it split from by over a
century.
