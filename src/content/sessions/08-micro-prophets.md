## Before the session

Pick one small splinter group not yet covered in lectures — the Cutlerites,
the Bickertonites, the Church of Christ (Temple Lot), or another — and read
enough to summarise its founding claim in three sentences.

## In the session

Alpheus Cutler split from the Reorganization decades after joining it, on his
own revelation. William Bickerton split from a Rigdonite remnant. The Temple
Lot church split from the Reorganization over the presidency's authority.
None of these founders knew each other, and all three repeat the same shape:
one person, one claimed revelation, one new church. We compare three small
cases side by side to test whether the four-part framework holds at scale,
not just for the well-documented majors.

## Afterwards

Bring the group you summarised — it's useful raw material for the comparative
session next week.
