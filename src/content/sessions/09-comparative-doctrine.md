## Before the session

Pick two branches studied so far and note one doctrine each kept from
Nauvoo-era teaching, and one it dropped.

## In the session

A side-by-side comparison: which branches kept temple endowment ordinances
(Utah, yes; the Reorganization, no, for decades); which kept plural marriage
(the FLDS, yes; almost everyone else, no); which kept the Book of Abraham as
scripture. Doctrinal retention tracks the founding pivot each group made in
weeks 2–8, not random drift — the pivot a group makes to justify its split is
usually also the doctrine it defends hardest afterward.

## Afterwards

This comparison is useful groundwork for the final project's map.
