## Before the session

Read a summary of Church of Christ (Temple Lot) v. Reorganized Church,
decided in 1894, over the Temple Lot property in Independence, Missouri.

## In the session

A federal court had to decide which church legally counted as Joseph Smith's
original organization, for property-title purposes — and found a narrow,
procedural answer that settled who owned a plot of land without settling, or
attempting to settle, who was theologically correct. We use the case to
separate two questions this course has been treating together: legal
legitimacy, and doctrinal legitimacy.
