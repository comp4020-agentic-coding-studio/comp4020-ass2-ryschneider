## Before the session

Read about the Reorganization's 1984 decision to ordain women, and the
"Restoration branches" that split off in response — the same church that
itself began as a splinter, splintering again a century later.

## In the session

The framework doesn't stop applying once a group settles down: a doctrinal
change creates a fresh vacuum for people who considered the earlier position
part of what made the Reorganization legitimate, and a new set of
independence groups forms in response. We also look briefly at present-day
independent fundamentalist groups, to confirm the pattern is still live
rather than a nineteenth-century artefact.
