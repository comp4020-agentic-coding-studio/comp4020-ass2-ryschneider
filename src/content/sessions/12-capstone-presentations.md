## Before the session

Have your schism map and written analysis in a state you're willing to show
the class, even if it isn't the version you submit.

## In the session

Short presentations, five minutes each, walking the class through one
interesting branch of your map. No new material — this session exists to let
the semester's dozen separate research threads meet each other before they're
marked separately.

## Afterwards

[The Schism Map](/assessments/final-project/) is due at the end of semester.
Nothing from this session is separately marked; it's here to make the last
two weeks of work visible to the room before it's submitted.
