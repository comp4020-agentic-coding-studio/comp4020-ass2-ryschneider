# The Fracturing of the Latter Day Saint Movement

## Week 1 — Before the Fracture

---

## Nauvoo, Illinois, 1844

- population rivalling Chicago's
- its own city charter, its own militia — the Nauvoo Legion
- Joseph Smith: mayor, lieutenant general, prophet, and (from January 1844)
  candidate for President of the United States

One man, four unrelated kinds of authority.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "Where is our safety? In the Priesthood."
>
> — attributed to Joseph Smith, 1844

---

## Four bodies with a claim

- the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles
- the Council of Fifty
- the surviving First Presidency
- Sidney Rigdon, sole remaining counselor

None of them had ever had to settle who outranked whom. That question had
never been asked while Smith was alive to answer it himself.

---

## Two secrets

- plural marriage, practised since the early 1840s, denied in public
- the fullness of temple ordinances, given only to the Twelve in a closed
  room in May 1844

Both become fault lines the moment the man holding them together is gone.
