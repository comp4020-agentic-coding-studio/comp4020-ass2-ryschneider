---

## The Morrill Act, 1862

- outlawed bigamy in U.S. territories
- barely enforced for two decades
- the Utah church treats it as background noise

---

## The Edmunds–Tucker Act, 1887

- disincorporated the church as a legal entity
- seized most of its property
- disenfranchised anyone practising or believing in plural marriage

Not a doctrinal argument. A legal one, applied directly to the institution's
ability to function.

## What changed, what didn't

- the church's public position: changed
- some members' private practice: didn't, for decades
- that gap is exactly where next week's fundamentalist split comes from
